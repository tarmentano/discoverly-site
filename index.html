From hearing to doing, in three steps

Most of us forget Sunday's message by Tuesday — not because we don't care, but because nobody showed us what to do with it. Discoverly turns the sermon into the plan for your week.

Step one
🎙️

Your pastor or priest preaches

Upload the sermon or homily audio after service. AI listens to the actual message — not a generic devotional — and drafts missions from its specific points. Parishes can even draft from Sunday's lectionary readings, shared by every parish worldwide.

Step two
✅

Your pastor approves

Staff review, edit, and publish five missions in minutes. Every mission traces to something actually said on Sunday — prayer, service, Scripture, community, and the historic works of mercy.

Step three
🧭

Everyone embarks together

Members check off missions through the week, build streaks, earn badges, and spur one another on through small-group challenges.

✝️

Grace first. Always.

Let's be clear about what Discoverly is — and what it isn't. Our relationship with God begins with His grace, received through faith in Christ — never earned by works. No mission, streak, or badge earns a single ounce of God's love. Nothing you complete in this app makes you more loved, and nothing you skip makes you less.

But grace doesn't stay on the couch. Scripture says we were created in Christ Jesus to do good works — not as the price of God's love, but as its fruit: faith expressing itself through love (Galatians 5:6). Discoverly exists to help you infuse your faith with works — to take what God said to your church on Sunday and let it change your Monday.

A habit as old as the Psalms

Believers have always written their walk down. David journaled his repentance — we call it Psalm 51. Augustine journaled his conversion — we call it the Confessions. Catholics have prayed the Daily Examen for five centuries: noticing, each evening, where God moved.

Discoverly stands in that lineage. Every mission ends with one line — "Where did you see God in this?" — and week by week those lines become a journal of grace you can actually re-read. Not a scoreboard for God. A record of what He did.
